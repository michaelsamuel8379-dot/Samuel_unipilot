WELCOME & BACKGROUND

About the Department

Based on publicly available AAUA Faculty of Agriculture information.

Welcome to Forestry and Wildlife Management at Adekunle Ajasin University (AAUA). The official Faculty of Agriculture lists the Department of Forestry and Wild Life Management under its Bachelor of Forestry programme, awarding B. Forestry and Wild Life Management.

What the field covers: forestry and wildlife management brings together the study of forest ecosystems, trees and vegetation, wildlife populations and habitats, biodiversity conservation, and sustainable management of natural resources.

Faculty context: AAUA's Faculty of Agriculture aims to prepare skilled agricultural graduates and support technical, productive, research and entrepreneurship capacity.

A current official department-specific programme handbook was not located during this research. Programme details that require the approved curriculum are clearly marked for verification.

LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT

Programme Objectives & Skills

The following are relevant learning areas for the discipline, not a verbatim AAUA departmental objective list. The official programme handbook should be used to confirm the department's exact outcomes.

  • Understand forest ecosystems, tree species, vegetation and relationships between organisms and their environment.
  • Build knowledge of wildlife ecology, habitat management, biodiversity and conservation principles.
  • Explore sustainable forest-resource planning, restoration and responsible use of natural resources.
  • Develop field observation, scientific data collection, analysis and research skills.
  • Understand the role of forestry and wildlife management in environmental protection and sustainable development.
  • Develop communication, teamwork, problem-solving and entrepreneurship skills relevant to natural-resource careers.

Potential subject areas

Forest resources

Forest ecology, tree identification, silviculture, forest inventory and resource management.

Wildlife & conservation

Wildlife ecology, biodiversity, habitat protection and conservation management.

Field research

Field surveys, environmental observation, data handling and evidence-based decision-making.

Sustainable development

Responsible resource use, restoration, community engagement and environmental stewardship.
